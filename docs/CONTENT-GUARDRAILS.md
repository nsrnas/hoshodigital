# Content guardrails

These rules apply to all seven WordPress templates.

## Source of truth

1. Approved HOSHŌ DIGITAL presentation and brand guides.
2. Existing corresponding HOSHŌ DIGITAL website page.
3. Approved `Figma Work/v9 Recontent Old Design` page.

The WordPress templates are generated from the seven active V9 HTML pages. `Figma Work/v9 Recontent Old Design/CONTENT-PROVENANCE.md` records the page-level content audit.

## Do not invent

Do not add or change these items without written approval:

- claims or performance promises;
- statistics, funding values, eligibility criteria, or timelines;
- step-by-step processes;
- programme scope or deliverables;
- partner relationships;
- testimonials, quotations, or customer names.

Design improvements may change layout, typography, motion, spacing, media treatment, and hierarchy while preserving validated meaning.

## Current image direction

- Use the approved V9 photography and brand assets.
- Do not add Hokusai or unrelated Japanese artwork.
- Do not repeat a content image across the seven pages.
- Every call-to-action section must use a relevant approved image.
- Every quotation must retain the approved portrait of the quoted person.
- Avoid unapproved AI-generated people.
- Keep production assets inside the theme; never link to Temp files or third-party images at runtime.

## Motion direction

- Motion must improve orientation, continuity, or hierarchy.
- Prefer transform and opacity; avoid layout-triggering scroll animation.
- Do not autoplay testimonial carousels.
- Keep interactions keyboard accessible.
- Always support `prefers-reduced-motion` and the static `?figma=1` capture state.
