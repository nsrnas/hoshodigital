# Coworker frame alignment

Source reviewed: the three `figma-to-html*.zip` exports in `Reference/`.

## Shared visual system

- Primary canvas: `#FFFFFF`; alternate canvas: `#F9F9FB`.
- Primary navy: `#071523`; supporting blue: `#0E2A47`.
- Primary red: `#E21C15`; deep red: `#AC0D07`.
- Primary ink: `#1A1C1D`; secondary copy: `#62646D`.
- Inter is the primary interface/display family; Arimo is the fallback/supporting family.
- Red is an accent or transition colour, not the default background for every section.
- Photography is full-bleed and unbordered. Hero imagery favours architectural or abstract red/black forms.
- Person quotes use one 471px horizontal treatment: white copy, burgundy-to-navy field, monochrome portrait, white attribution.
- Sections rely on whitespace and subtle dividers before cards, shadows, or decorative geometry.

## Seven-page consistency rules

- Reuse the shared header, hero, quote, CTA, footer, button, and spacing tokens.
- Keep hero copy vertically centred and left aligned; supporting copy stays below the headline.
- Keep body copy at readable contrast; do not place muted navy text on red.
- Keep one dominant visual per section and avoid repeated photography between pages.
- Reserve pale pink (`#F9D2D0`) for small quote marks or minor accents only.
