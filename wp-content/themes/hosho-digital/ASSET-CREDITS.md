## Company values portraits

The Company values carousel uses locally optimized WebP adaptations of the following public-domain or CC0 sources:

- `company-values/courageous-aristotle.webp`: [Aristotle (transparent)](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Aristotle_(transparent).png), S. Perquin, CC0 1.0.
- `company-values/curious-voltaire.webp`: [Portrait of Voltaire, LACMA M.78.7](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Portrait_of_Voltaire_(Francois-Marie_Arouet_(1694_-_1778))_LACMA_M.78.7_(6_of_6).jpg), Los Angeles County Museum of Art, public domain.
- `company-values/accountable-moliere.webp`: [Moliere2](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Moliere2.jpg), public-domain reproduction of a portrait of Molière.
- `company-values/human-tolstoy.webp`: [Leo Tolstoy, portrait](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Leo_Tolstoy,_portrait.jpg), Sass, Moscow / Library of Congress, public domain.
- `company-values/kaizen-van-gogh.webp`: [VanGogh 1887 Selbstbildnis](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:VanGogh_1887_Selbstbildnis.jpg), public-domain reproduction of Vincent van Gogh's 1887 self-portrait.

Adaptation: each source was cropped to a consistent portrait ratio, resized to 1200 × 1400 pixels, converted to WebP, and presented in monochrome through CSS.
